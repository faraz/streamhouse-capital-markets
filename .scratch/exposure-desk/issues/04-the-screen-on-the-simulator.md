# 04: The screen on the simulator

**What to build:** The whole demo, working offline. Every beat of the run-of-show
driveable with no network and no credentials. When this ticket closes, the deterministic
stage fallback is finished and the talk is safe regardless of what happens to the live
stack.

Port the prototype onto the seam rather than rewriting it: its behaviour has already been
reviewed and approved, and its numbers are the fixture. The screen renders a snapshot and
holds no business logic of its own.

**Blocked by:** 03.
